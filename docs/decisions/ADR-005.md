# ADR-005: Reusable Workflow Infrastructure

- **Status:** PROPOSED
- **Date:** 2026-11-13 (baseline documentation)
- **Decision owner:** Not recorded

## Context and problem

Workflow concepts exist in domain-specific code such as service logic and `MROWorkflowStep`. A generic engine can reduce duplication but can also hide critical business rules in opaque configuration.

## Proposed direction

Do not build reusable workflow machinery until multiple stable, repeated patterns justify it. Keep business lifecycle rules explicit and typed; do not place arbitrary workflow logic in unvalidated JSON settings.

## Consequences and open decisions

Inventory repeated transitions, versioning needs, observability, permission semantics, and domain ownership first. An ADR detailing scope and trade-offs is required before an engine is proposed for implementation. No engine is approved by this record.

## Alternatives

- Build a generic engine immediately: rejected as premature absent verified repeated requirements.
- Keep all transitions domain-specific: acceptable until repetition and operational requirements are evidenced.
