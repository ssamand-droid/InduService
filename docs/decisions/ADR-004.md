# ADR-004: Business State and Financial State

- **Status:** PROPOSED
- **Date:** 2026-11-13 (baseline documentation)
- **Decision owner:** Not recorded

## Context

Payment models include invoices, payments, transactions, wallets, commissions, and settlements and depend on service/order concepts. A completed job, accepted work, invoice, payment, and settlement describe different facts.

## Proposed direction

Maintain separate business and financial state machines. Technical completion or customer acceptance alone does not mark payment/settlement complete; a financial record alone does not establish technical acceptance.

## Consequences and open decisions

Define authoritative financial events, idempotency, refunds/reversals, reconciliation, currency/rounding, payer/payee, and audit requirements. Preserve historical financial facts and avoid destructive recalculation. Requires finance/domain-owner approval and API impact review.

## Alternatives

- One shared lifecycle/status: rejected because independent events can occur in different orders and need separate auditability.
- Separate linked business and finance records: proposed direction.
