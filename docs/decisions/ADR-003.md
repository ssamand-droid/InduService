# ADR-003: Canonical Product and Seller Offer

- **Status:** PROPOSED
- **Date:** 2026-11-13 (baseline documentation)
- **Decision owner:** Not recorded

## Context

The `products` app contains canonical catalog concepts. Seller-specific price, availability, MOQ, lead time, warranty, origin, and condition need distinct ownership. A first-class offer model was not found in the inspected inventory.

## Proposed direction

Keep `Product` as canonical catalog identity and represent seller-specific commercial terms in a separate offer concept if product requirements justify it.

## Consequences and open decisions

Inventory existing fields, product search/filter behavior, cart/checkout, order snapshots, and historical data before deciding whether/how to migrate. Decide offer ownership, status, validity, multi-currency behavior, and whether procurement is in initial scope. No model/API is approved during the freeze.

## Alternatives

- Retain seller terms directly on Product: lowest migration cost but muddles canonical facts and changing offers.
- Separate offer from product: clearer ownership; adds lifecycle and migration complexity.
