# ADR-006: Asset as an Operational History Root

- **Status:** PROPOSED
- **Date:** 2026-11-13 (baseline documentation)
- **Decision owner:** Not recorded

## Context

The assets app contains asset, part, document, maintenance, sensor, alert, schedule, hotspot, and prediction concepts. The architecture plan aims to connect maintenance history, work, parts, evidence, failures, and costs. Duplicate hotspot representations and incomplete organizational hierarchy are audit findings.

## Proposed direction

Use the asset as the anchor for its authorized operational history, while defining organizational ownership/hierarchy explicitly (organization/site/area/line/asset as appropriate). Keep parts, documents, failures, maintenance events, costs, and execution references linked without merging their domain ownership.

## Consequences and open decisions

Verify current relationships, data quality, tenant ownership, and history retention. Decide hierarchy levels and rationalize `Hotspot` versus `DigitalTwinHotspot` before schema changes. Prediction claims require provenance and validation evidence. No new asset models are approved during Phase 0.

## Alternatives

- Flat assets with ad hoc references: lower immediate cost but weaker plant context/history.
- Explicit hierarchy and linked operational records: proposed direction, pending owner approval and migration analysis.
